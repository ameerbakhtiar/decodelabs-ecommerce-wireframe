# 🛒 E-Commerce Gaming Storefront — Low-Fidelity Wireframe

**Organization:** Decode Labs  
**Phase:** Project 2 — Information Architecture & Wireframing

---

## 📌 Problem Overview
E-commerce brands often experience customer drop-offs during browsing and checkout due to cluttered layouts and unclear visual hierarchy. This project focuses on establishing a clean, logical homepage structure ("The Skeleton") that guides users seamlessly through purchasing pathways before introducing visual design elements.

---

## 🏗️ Structural Breakdown
Designed a complete, top-to-bottom layout hierarchy tailored for a high-performance gaming storefront:
* **Navigation Header:** Clean brand anchor, categories, search functionality, and direct access to the shopping cart.
* **Hero Section:** Clear value proposition paired with a prominent primary Call-to-Action (CTA) button (`SHOP NOW`).
* **Trust Badges:** Highlighting key selling points (Free Shipping, 2-Year Warranty, 24/7 Support) to build immediate user confidence.
* **Featured Product Grid:** Standardized product cards equipped with titles, pricing, and secondary CTAs (`Add to Cart`).
* **Promotional Banner & Footer:** High-contrast mid-page promotion with structured footer navigation and newsletter subscription.

---

## 📐 Design Rules & Constraints
* **Strict Greyscale Palette:** Limited strictly to Black, White, and Grey shades to test information structure purely through contrast and position.
* **Image Placeholders:** Standardized geometric placeholders (`X`-box markers) used in place of high-resolution imagery.
* **Clear CTAs:** Solid, high-contrast button elements to establish clear primary vs. secondary action hierarchy.

---
